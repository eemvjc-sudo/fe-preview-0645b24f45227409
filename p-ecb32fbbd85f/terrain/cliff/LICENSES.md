# Twin Ridges cliff, ridge and rock textures

Photo-scanned PBR maps packed into `cliff_albedo.jpg`, `cliff_normal.jpg` (OpenGL Y-up), `cliff_rough.jpg`, and `cliff_ao.jpg` by `client/tools/terrain/pack_cliff.py`. The atlas is a 2×2 grid (jointed cliff face, cracked boulder, rock face, rock ground / scree), 1008 px tiles with 8 px wrap padding in 1024 px cells (2048 px atlas). Albedo tiles are graded to a shared grey-tan.

All of the following are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No BAR or Recoil assets are included.
