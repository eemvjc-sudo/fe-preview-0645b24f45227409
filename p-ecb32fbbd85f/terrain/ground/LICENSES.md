# Twin Ridges ground textures

Photo-scanned PBR maps packed into `ground_albedo.jpg`, `ground_normal.jpg` (OpenGL Y-up), and `ground_rough.jpg` by `client/tools/terrain/pack_ground.py`. The atlas is a 2×2 grid (meadow grass, forest floor, dry soil, rock), 1008 px tiles with 8 px wrap padding in 1024 px cells (2048 px atlas).

All of the following are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Only the 2D maps were used; accompanying 3D scenes or models were not imported. No BAR or Recoil assets are included.
